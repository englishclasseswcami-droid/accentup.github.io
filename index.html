AccentUp is designed around a unique 3-step framework that shifts you from just "knowing" English to actually owning it.

🧏
STEP 1

HEAR IT

Decode the real language. Before you can say a sound, you need to truly hear it. We analyze the English language through its sound system using materials you actually enjoy—your favorite series, movies, TED Talks, or songs.

🌐
STEP 2

OWN IT

Intentional practice. This is where the physical training happens. We practice real-world pronunciation by diving deep into connected speech, linking, and natural intonation through intensive shadowing techniques.

👄
STEP 3

SAY IT

Automate your speech. We simulate your real professional life: practicing upcoming presentations, simulating high-stakes meetings, and role-playing interviews. Speak under pressure without getting stuck.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this program right for beginners?

No. AccentUp is exclusively designed for professionals who already have an intermediate or advanced understanding of English grammar and vocabulary, but struggle with oral fluency, pronunciation, and confidence.

I have a very busy schedule. How much time do I need to invest?

The program is built for busy professionals. Alongside our highly focused 60-minute weekly live session, you will get a personalized routine using our 24/7 interactive platform. You don't need hours a day—you just need consistent, intentional practice (even 15 minutes a day makes a huge difference).

This is a highly personalized, 1-on-1 mentorship. I work directly with you, using your own real-world materials (like your upcoming presentations or meetings) to tackle your specific blind spots.
